# 5. Security Operations

## The SOC
- Roles: Tier 1 analyst, Tier 2/3 responder, threat hunter, detection engineer, SOC manager.
- Tooling: SIEM, EDR/XDR, SOAR, threat-intel platforms, ticketing.

## AI in the SOC
- Alert triage and noise reduction; automated enrichment.
- Natural-language log queries and rule generation.
- SOAR playbooks with AI-assisted decisions.
- Metrics: mean time to detect (MTTD), mean time to respond (MTTR), false-positive rate.

## Risks
- Over-reliance on automation; unreviewed AI output; sensitive data sent to external models.
- Need for governance: approved tools, data handling rules, audit trails.
