# 4. Incident Response

**Theory time: 40 min** · Hands-on: [lab 04](../labs/04-incident-response/README.md)

## Lifecycle (NIST SP 800-61)
1. Preparation
2. Detection and analysis
3. Containment, eradication, recovery
4. Post-incident activity (lessons learned)

## Where AI helps
- Triage and prioritization, enrichment of indicators, timeline reconstruction.
- Drafting incident summaries and stakeholder communication.
- Suggested playbook steps, with analyst approval.

## Good practice
- Preserve evidence; document every action with timestamps.
- Automate containment only for well-understood, low-risk actions.
- Run tabletop exercises and review after every incident.

## Exercise idea
- Walk through a phishing-to-credential-theft scenario: what do you detect, contain, and communicate, and in what order?
