# 1. AI in Cybersecurity

**Theory time: 45 min** · Hands-on: [lab 01](../labs/01-ai-in-cybersecurity/README.md)

## Why it matters
- Attack volume and alert counts exceed what human analysts can triage manually.
- AI/ML adds speed and pattern recognition; humans keep judgment and context.

## Key ideas
- Rules/signatures vs. machine learning: strengths and blind spots of each.
- Supervised (known malware, phishing classifiers) vs. unsupervised (anomaly detection) learning.
- Common use cases: phishing and spam filtering, malware classification, user behavior analytics (UEBA), fraud detection, vulnerability prioritization.
- Limitations: false positives, data drift, lack of labeled attack data, explainability.
- Adversarial ML: evasion, data poisoning, model extraction.

## Discussion
- Where would you trust a model's verdict automatically, and where would you keep a human in the loop?
